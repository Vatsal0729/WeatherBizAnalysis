02 / PREPROCESSING & AUDITING · CO 2

Keep data cleaning decisions completely visible.

  1. Ingest raw transaction-level payloads without altering source schemas; validate datetimes and IANA local timezones.
  2. Exclude records with negative values, zero-duration rides (dock testing false starts), and duplicate primary keys.
  3. For online retail, separate credit invoice cancellations (invoice 'C' series) into a returns ledger to avoid distorting gross demand.
  4. Verify SEC corporate quarterly statements against 10-K audited annual balance sheets, resolving retrospective restatements.
  5. Spatial & temporal alignment: merge weather measures on exact hourly and daily intervals without synthetic interpolation.

No blanket outlier deletion is applied. Extreme revenue spikes and heavy downpours are preserved as legitimate operational evidence.

06 / PRESENTATION TAKEAWAYS

Present conclusions supported by empirical evidence.

Recommended Presentation Sequence

  1. State the data source, geographic coverage, time horizon, and operational unit of measure.
  2. Explain preprocessing exclusions and data accounting transparently.
  3. Contrast mean against median to highlight skewness and extreme tail events.
  4. Compare precipitation bins alongside day-of-week baselines.
  5. Formulate defensible empirical elasticities; distinguish correlation from causation.
